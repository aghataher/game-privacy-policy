GBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026
App: GBox
Package: com.mrtaher.gbox
Developer: mrtaher
Contact: amir.agahataher.dev@gmail.com

1. What this page covers

GBox is a collection of online and offline board and card games. This policy describes the data processed by the Android app com.mrtaher.gbox. By installing or using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information described on this page. If you do not agree, do not install or use the app.

2. Information we collect

Account. You may play as a guest. A guest account is tied to the device and receives a name such as Guest-xxxx. Registration requires a username, password, and email. We also store a display name, avatar, language, and an optional invite code. Passwords are stored only as a cryptographic hash, not as plain text.

Gameplay. We store match results, wins and losses, points, league, wallet balances (coins, gems, and tickets), purchased items, wheel spins, daily rewards, friends, online status, and last seen time. We also store lobby chat, preset in-match phrases, free-text chat available with a subscription, support tickets, gift codes, and invite codes.

Device and connection. For login, abuse prevention, and diagnostics we receive a device identifier, device model, operating system, device type, language, time zone, screen size, app version, game-engine version, approximate hardware details (memory, processor, graphics, battery, network type), the store the app was installed from, and the IP address observed by our server.

Purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number. We keep the purchase token to deliver items and to prevent the same purchase from being reused.

Ads. The app shows ads, including through Tapsell and Adivery. Those services may read the Android advertising ID. You can reset or limit that ID in your device’s Google settings.

We do not request precise location, contacts, gallery photos, microphone, camera, or SMS.

3. How we use information

  • Create and keep accounts, sign-in, and session restore
  • Run online play, friends, rankings, chat, and support
  • Operate the in-app wallet, shop, and purchase checks
  • Show ads and grant rewarded-ad prizes
  • Prevent cheating, extra accounts on one device, and chat abuse, and suspend accounts that break the rules
  • Fix bugs, check device compatibility, and send in-app messages
  • Comply with law and valid legal requests

We do not sell personal information for advertising outside the ad networks used inside the app.

4. Who we share it with

RecipientWhatWhy
Our own serversAccount, gameplay, device, chat, purchasesRun the service
Google PlayYour Google account and purchase receipt, on Google’s sideInstall, payment, and Google policies
Ad networks, including Tapsell, Adivery, and their partnersAdvertising ID and technical ad dataShow and measure ads
Other playersDisplay name, avatar, online status, public stats, and chat you sendPlay, friends, and the lobby
AuthoritiesOnly what the law requiresAnswer a valid order or prevent abuse

Chat is visible to players in the same channel. Do not post your password, email, or purchase receipt in chat.

5. Children

GBox is not directed to children under 13. The app includes accounts, chat, in-app purchases, and ads. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that such an account exists, we will delete it. A parent can contact us at the email below.

6. Retention and security

We keep account and gameplay data while the account is active. A sign-in token stays on the device so you do not have to log in every time. Signing out invalidates that session. Purchase tokens, suspension records, and security logs may be kept longer when needed for a payment dispute, fraud prevention, or a legal duty, including after account deletion. Lobby chat has a history limit and is not kept forever. Traffic to our servers is encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices

  • You can stay a guest and not create an account.
  • You can change your display name, email, password, and avatar in your profile.
  • You can choose not to use chat.
  • You can reset or limit the advertising ID in Android settings.
  • To delete your account, correct your data, or receive a copy, email amir.agahataher.dev@gmail.com with your username. We will do this after we match the request to the account, unless the law or an open payment dispute requires us to keep something.

Uninstalling the app does not delete the account on our server. Deletion requires a request.

8. International processing

Servers may be located outside the country where you live. By using the app, you accept that transfer as part of running the service.

9. Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the new version.

10. Contact

Email: amir.agahataher.dev@gmail.com
