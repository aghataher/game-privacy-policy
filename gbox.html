GBox is a collection of online and offline board and card games. This policy describes how that app handles information. By installing or using GBox, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Account. You can play as a guest. A guest account is tied to the device. Creating an account requires a username, a password, and an email address. We also store a display name, avatar, language, and an optional invite code. Passwords are stored only as a cryptographic hash.

Gameplay. We store match results, wins and losses, points, league, wallet balances, purchased items, friends, online status, and last seen time. We store lobby chat, preset chat phrases, free-text chat sent with a subscription, support messages, gift codes, and invite codes.

Device. We receive a device identifier, device model, operating system, language, time zone, screen size, app version, approximate hardware details, the store the app was installed from, and the IP address seen by our server. We use these for sign-in, abuse prevention, and diagnostics.

Purchases. Google Play processes payments. We do not receive your card number. We keep the purchase token to deliver items and to stop the same purchase from being used twice.

Ads. GBox shows ads through Tapsell, Adivery, and their partners. Those services may read the Android advertising ID. You can reset or limit that ID in your device’s Google settings.

We do not request precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or SMS.

How we use information

  • Run accounts, sign-in, and session restore
  • Run online play, friends, rankings, chat, and support
  • Run the in-app wallet, shop, and purchase checks
  • Show ads and grant rewarded-ad prizes
  • Prevent cheating, extra accounts, and chat abuse
  • Fix bugs and meet legal duties

We do not sell personal information.

Children

GBox is not directed to children under 13. It includes accounts, chat, purchases, and ads. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that such an account exists, we delete it.

Retention and security

Account and gameplay data stay while the account is active. Signing out invalidates the session stored on the device. Purchase tokens, suspension records, and security logs may be kept longer when needed for a payment dispute, fraud prevention, or a legal duty. Lobby chat is kept only up to a limited history. Traffic to our servers is encrypted. Uninstalling the app does not delete the account on the server.

Your choices

You can play as a guest, skip chat, and limit the advertising ID in Android settings. To correct your data, receive a copy, or delete the account, email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your username. Deletion of the app from the phone does not delete the server account.

Changes

If these practices change, this page and the date above will be updated. Continuing to use GBox after an update means you accept the new policy.
