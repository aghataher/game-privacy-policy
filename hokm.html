1. What this page covers

This policy describes the Android app حکم 4 نفره (package com.Aghataher.hokm), a four-player Hokm card game played on this device against the computer. It covers this app only. The app does not create a player account, and it does not offer chat.

2. Information we collect

The app stores the following on the device, in the app’s private storage. This app does not send it to a server operated by mrtaher:

  • Names you type for the four seats. If you do not change them, the app uses a default label for you and generated names for the other seats.
  • Match progress and results, including tricks, hands, the declarer, and the win type, kept so the statistics screens can show them.
  • A coin balance stored on the device. The balance is encrypted in local storage.
  • Settings you choose, such as music on or off, volume, card back, table theme, and how cards are played.
  • Counters for how often the store-rating prompt and the other-app prompt have been shown, and whether you asked not to see them again.

On Android, the app loads Adivery ads (a banner and an optional rewarded video). When an ad is requested or shown, the Adivery SDK processes technical data needed to deliver and measure that ad. That can include the advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions such as a view, a click, or a completed rewarded video. This app does not send your seat names, coin balance, or match statistics to Adivery, and it does not set an Adivery user id.

Optional coin packs are purchased through Cafe Bazaar. Cafe Bazaar receives the product identifier and completes the payment with the Bazaar account on the device. This app receives only whether the purchase succeeded or failed, then adds the coins to the balance stored on the device. It does not receive your card number, national ID, or Bazaar password.

If you tap Share, Android opens the system share sheet with a short text and a Cafe Bazaar link for this app. The app does not read the app you pick or the person you send it to. Links that open a Cafe Bazaar page or a public webpage leave this app only when you tap them.

The app manifest requests Internet access and permission to pay through Cafe Bazaar. A plugin manifest also includes permission to check whether a network connection is available. The manifest does not request precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or SMS. The game does not ask you for an email address or a phone number.

3. How we use information

Local names, scores, statistics, coins, and settings are used only to run the game, show statistics, remember preferences, and apply a coin purchase or a rewarded-ad result on the device. Store-prompt counters are used only to decide when to show those prompts.

Adivery uses ad data to fill the banner and the rewarded video, to limit repeat requests, and to measure whether an ad was shown, clicked, or rewarded. A completed rewarded video reduces the opposing side’s hand count in the current match. Cafe Bazaar uses purchase data to charge you and to confirm the product. We use the success or failure result only to update the local coin balance.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We do not operate a server for this app, and we do not sell personal information. The app shares data with these parties only for the features below:

  • Adivery, to request, show, and measure the banner and rewarded video on Android.
  • Cafe Bazaar, to process an optional coin purchase and when you open a Cafe Bazaar store page from the app.

Seat names, match statistics, settings, and the coin balance stay on the device. They are not uploaded by this app. A share is handled by the Android app you choose.

5. Children

حکم 4 نفره is not directed to children under 13. It is a general card game with advertising and optional purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information to us, email amir.agahataher.dev@gmail.com.

6. Retention and security

Names, statistics, settings, prompt counters, and the coin balance remain on the device until you change or clear them, or until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Starting a new match from the main flow clears the saved match statistics on the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes this local data, including the coin balance stored in the app. There is no account on our servers to keep or delete.

The coin balance is encrypted before it is saved in local storage. Other local values are kept in the app’s private storage. No method of storage is perfectly secure. Adivery and Cafe Bazaar keep information they process under their own retention and security practices. We do not keep a copy of ad requests or payments.

7. Your choices

You can change the seat names and the game settings in the app. You can skip a rewarded video. Coin purchases are optional. You can dismiss the store prompts, including choosing not to show a prompt again. You do not have to use Share.

To delete the names, statistics, settings, prompt counters, and coin balance stored by this app, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Because this app has no server account, there is no profile on our side to erase. Cafe Bazaar may still keep a purchase record in your Bazaar account under Cafe Bazaar’s own policy. You can reset or limit use of the Android advertising identifier in your device settings; that choice affects ads served by Adivery.

To ask a privacy question or to tell us about information you believe we hold, email amir.agahataher.dev@gmail.com.

8. International processing

mrtaher does not transfer game data to a server of our own. Adivery and Cafe Bazaar process ad and purchase information on their own systems. Those companies operate primarily in Iran, and they may process data in Iran or in other countries where their service providers operate. Their processing is covered by their own privacy policies.

9. Changes

If this app’s data practices change, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above. The new text applies from the date shown.
